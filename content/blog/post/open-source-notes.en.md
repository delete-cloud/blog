Am I utilitarian about open source? Honestly, somewhat. To build influence, among other things. But at the same time, I enjoy the process of getting a PR merged, and I enjoy my work being used by others.

After being laid off, I've been looking for things to do. Right now I don't really want to interview for business roles at big companies, so contributing to open source became — you could say — an escape. But it genuinely brings me some spiritual satisfaction.

Either way, I think I do love open source: the software, the interactions with people in the community, having my contributions used and recognized.

It's what keeps me going.
